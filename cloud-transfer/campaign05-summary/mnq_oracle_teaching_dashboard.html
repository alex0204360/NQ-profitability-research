What the agent “brain” can show

1 · Completed chartOHLCV history and causal market state, available after the minute closes.
2 · Student valuesPredicted net dollars for eight entry/exit choices. Abstain if none clears stressed costs.
3 · Decision traceTimestamp, feature values, eight predictions, selected action, horizon, and abstention reason. Local feature sensitivities can explain a selected model’s forecast; they are not literal thoughts.
4 · Oracle lesson laterOnly after the future path matures, reveal all eight realized values and compare with the student. Missing bars invalidate the label.

The hindsight oracle sees future entry and exit prices. Its high positive-action rate measures how often a perfect hindsight choice exists, not how often a student can predict it. Exact oracle imitation is shown below, but ranking and executable replay determine whether any candidate is useful.

How to read this result

A rank score above zero means higher predicted action values tended to correspond to better realized action values in that fold. The fixed gate requires all three fold scores above zero and a mean above 0.05. Scores were chosen without replay profit. A winner gets exactly one internal holdout replay; that older holdout is still development evidence. Production requires a newly collected, source-audited cohort and a live feature adapter that reproduces the same inputs.

Historical future bars cannot tell us which future state will occur next. The teacher compares eight fixed actions on the one path that actually happened; it does not predict every possible path. A future student could model a distribution of scenarios, but its probabilities and payoffs would still need calibration and prospective validation before a live chart could use them.
