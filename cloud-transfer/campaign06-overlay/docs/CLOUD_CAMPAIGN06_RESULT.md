# Campaign 06: human-style chart structure and probabilistic entries

**The fixed probability-gated policy correctly abstained; it did not establish profitability.** The registered [plan](CLOUD_CAMPAIGN06.json) translated chart reading into two causal setups: a retest of confirmed horizontal support/resistance in the EMA trend, and a retest of a line projected from two confirmed pivots. A pivot required two later completed candles before it could influence a decision. Entry was at the next open after the retest candle. The primary exit was ten bars later. No outcome or future pivot entered a chart feature.

The gate learned each setup and direction from earlier, fully matured, gap-free September examples. It used the worse of immediate and one-bar-delayed stressed net, then required at least 20 examples, positive empirical mean net, and a conservative Beta posterior win probability above the payoff-implied breakeven probability. Horizontal longs were the closest: 73 examples, 39 wins, posterior mean win probability 53.2%, conservative lower probability **46.0%**, and breakeven probability **50.0%**. Their mean robust net was only +$1.86 per opportunity. The lower probability did not clear the payoff threshold. Horizontal shorts had 56 examples and -$17.03 mean robust net; trendline longs and shorts had just 19 and 13 examples with negative mean net. All four setup/direction combinations were blocked before evaluation.

The raw rule is a predeclared descriptive control, not a qualified candidate. Its immediate stressed session-bootstrap lower expectancy was negative on the eight-session original (+$317.50 total) and 14-session upload (-$511.50 total). The newer +$480 result has only two sessions and 17 trades. Gap-exit stressed net was +$50 on the original eight-session window, -$95.50 on the 14-session upload, and $0 on the newer window. The unchanged $8 gate earned +$196.50 immediately but -$1,250 delayed on the same 14-session upload, so neither policy showed reliable delay robustness there. The registered ATR bracket sensitivity made no trades because the gate blocked every entry.

The [private chart](/workspace/outputs/mnq_human_chart_PRIVATE.png) plots the levels available at each completed minute for an October 13 short retest near confirmed resistance. Its next-open ten-bar trade lost **$95** under stressed costs; the probability gate abstained. The [dashboard](/workspace/outputs/mnq_human_chart_dashboard_PRIVATE.html) shows the calculation, all fixed windows, and this chart. The published aggregate dashboard and JSON contain no raw price series.

Support and resistance summarize past trades; a touch can precede a bounce or a break. A probability-informed entry is only useful if its estimated payoff remains positive after uncertainty, slippage, fees, delay, and position occupancy. Here the apparently positive training horizontal-long average lacked enough confidence, while raw chart trades reversed across source windows. All periods were already inspected in earlier research, the Yahoo contract/roll and minute convention remain unverified, and no finite live loss budget has been agreed. Raw 2025 remained sealed; no broker was connected and no orders were submitted. Future work requires a fixed rule tested on new source-audited observations rather than adjusting these thresholds to the observed winners.
