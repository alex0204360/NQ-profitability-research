How the chart rule thinks

1 · Plot completed candlesBuild EMA trend, ATR, and nearest confirmed support/resistance. Confirm a pivot only after two later candles close.
2 · Wait for a retestPrice touches a horizontal level or projected two-pivot trendline within 0.25 ATR, then closes back in the trend direction.
3 · Price the riskUse earlier matured examples to estimate win probability, mean win, mean loss, and the breakeven probability after stressed costs and fill delay.
4 · Enter or abstainTrade only if the conservative probability bound beats breakeven and historical mean net is positive. Here, every setup abstained.

What this says about predicting a chart

A trendline is a summary of past prices, not a physical path the market must follow. A support touch can precede either a bounce or a break. Historical future bars can teach the probabilities of those outcomes; they cannot reveal which one a live chart will take. This run made that uncertainty explicit and abstained when the conservative probability did not beat the observed payoff threshold.
