Why a larger newer result is not the answer

The newer tree student reported +$1,091 immediately, but one +$2,647 trade exited at the next observed open after nine missing minutes. Removing that unverifiable trade as a sensitivity calculation leaves -$1,556. The newer multi-horizon student reported +$1,379, including +$2,921 across four gap exits; removing them leaves -$1,542. Removing trades after seeing outcomes is not a tradable rule.

What the confluence test taught us

Requiring agreement between a numerical forecast and a human-style level retest reduced the earlier window from 455 $8 signals to six confirmations and two filled trades. Those two earned +$91 immediately and +$50 delayed, but came from only two active sessions. In the newer window, two filled trades came from one active session. The fixed qualification screen requires at least 50 trades, ten active sessions, positive stressed and extreme outcomes under both fills, and an untouched source-audited confirmation period. None of these policies passed it. Changing the three-bar agreement window after seeing these outcomes would make the comparison misleading.
