# Campaign 08: control theory and drift–diffusion on the frozen $8 gate

**Reject the physics-inspired filters.** The fixed [plan](CLOUD_CAMPAIGN08.json) tested two ways to require more evidence before taking the unchanged $8-gate signal. Two-bar persistence was a causal hysteresis/debounce rule. The diffusion rule estimated the mean and volatility of the last 31 completed one-minute price changes, included uncertainty in estimated drift, and required a modeled probability above 60% of clearing $6 stressed costs over ten minutes. Their intersection was the registered primary combination. The frozen $8 signal, costs, ten-bar exit, and causal next-open replay did not change. All baseline trade counts and net results matched the frozen results exactly before filter scoring.

The probability hypothesis failed before economic selection. On 450 mature contiguous $8-gate opportunities in that window, its average forecast probability was **63.9%**, while the observed positive stressed outcome rate was **39.6%**. Its Brier error was **0.408**, worse than **0.239** for a constant forecast at the observed base rate. In the model's 80–100% bin, the mean forecast was 85.9%, yet only 35.3% of outcomes were positive. This is overconfidence, not a discoverable physical law. All three filters lost money under both fill timings; none is retained. The newer two-session upload also favored the unchanged gate in total net (+$429 immediately / +$259.50 delayed); the diffusion-only rule made +$269.50 / +$233 with just 19 trades, while the combined rule lost $51.50 / $244.

The updated goal specifies a **$25,000 account and $1,500 maximum permitted firm drawdown**, a 6% account reference. The campaign was preregistered before that instruction arrived, so the threshold was not used to select or alter a candidate. The combined filter's research bar-close drawdown of $1,902.50 on the 14-session window equals **7.61%** of the starting account and exceeds the $1,500 reference; its delayed drawdown was $2,040.50. The unchanged gate's delayed bar-close drawdown was $1,657, also above the reference. These research figures do not implement a firm's actual trailing-drawdown mechanics. No firm or exact rule has been provided, and a ten-bar time exit has no guaranteed per-trade loss bound, so account survival is not established.

The [dashboard](/workspace/outputs/mnq_physics_filter_dashboard.html) shows every fixed candidate, the delay/cost outcomes, drawdowns, and predicted-versus-observed probability bins. All data windows were previously inspected and labeled Yahoo MNQ without independent contract, roll, or minute-convention verification. No threshold was tuned after observing these failures. Raw 2025 stayed sealed; no broker was connected and no orders were submitted.

The single highest-impact obstacle is the lack of **verified, stable causal predictive information**. Repeatedly filtering the same noisy minute-bar forecast has not improved either calibration or net economics. The next experiment should start with a contract-identified, append-only post-freeze cohort and test whether new causal information improves prediction over a base-rate and frozen $8 baseline *before* optimizing another policy.
