# Why the MNQ students did not learn profitable decisions

The frozen dual-delay students did not rank future ten-minute MNQ outcomes on the already opened October 13–29 upload. [`cloud_learning_diagnostic.py`](../tools/cloud_learning_diagnostic.py) verifies the immutable campaign and upload hashes, aligns every prediction with its completed candle, excludes incomplete and gapped future windows, and compares predictions with both next-open and one-bar-delayed outcomes. It does not fit or tune a policy. The aggregate JSON is `outputs/mnq_learning_diagnostic.json` after running `python -m tools.cloud_learning_diagnostic`.

Ridge selected 1,087 mature opportunities; extra trees selected 2,037. These are overlapping hypothetical opportunities, not independent filled trades. The frozen causal replay actually filled 181 ridge trades and 301 tree trades; its stressed immediate nets were -$1,832 and -$837. The extra-trees realized means across ascending predicted-score quintiles were +$2.66, -$2.15, -$3.44, -$3.06, and -$0.89 before trading costs for the immediate fill. The top score group was not best. Prediction errors averaged about $46 per opportunity against a $6 stressed round-trip cost, so simply raising confidence or adding tree depth has no demonstrated basis.

The gap audit also found that the apparent +$1,091 tree profit on the newer two-session upload depended on one +$2,647 trade exited at the first observed open after nine missing minutes. A sensitivity calculation without that unverifiable fill is -$1,556. This is a source/execution problem as well as a modeling problem. The Yahoo CSV's exact contract, roll, and timestamp convention remain unverified. The $8 gate's +$196.50 stressed immediate net over 14 October sessions turned into -$1,250 with a one-bar delay. None of these results qualifies a live strategy.

## A teaching method that can be tested honestly

[`cloud_robust_teacher.py`](../tools/cloud_robust_teacher.py) now produces **both** long and short net targets for immediate and one-bar-delayed fills at a fixed ten-bar exit. Each target includes the $6 stressed round-trip cost; labels exist only if both futures matured before the cutoff in one contiguous segment. The hindsight class is long, short, or abstain according to the worse of the two fill timings. All losing alternatives stay in the training table. The hindsight class is **never** a live input. The fact that a hindsight teacher finds a winner on about three quarters of observed minutes measures lookahead opportunity, not learnability.

The next bounded student should estimate the *distribution* of each action's realized net, then abstain unless a calibrated lower bound on **both** fill timings clears zero. Train on an earlier chronological block, calibrate on a later purged block, and choose the policy once before collecting a new untouched cohort. A session-grouped calibration check should compare predicted and realized payoff by score bucket; a model with no positive rank ordering or no robust positive bucket is rejected before replay. Replay must include position occupancy, next-open fills, gap cancellation, stressed and extreme costs, one-bar delay, and session-level concentration. Add a richer feature only when the same timestamped value can be derived from the append-only live feed and historical data; potential candidates are verified contract-specific volume/order-book imbalance and scheduled-event context known at decision time. Data provenance and parity take priority over model complexity.

This is a research protocol, not a claim of improved profit. The current October and November observations have already been inspected, so they cannot confirm the next model. The next confirmation cohort needs independently identified MNQ contracts, exchange timestamps with start/end-minute convention, an explicit roll rule, completeness checks, and append-only observations collected after the policy is frozen. Compare with the unchanged $8 gate and no-trade control on identical sessions. The previously registered minimums of 50 trades and 10 active sessions, positive stressed results under both fill timings and a positive session-bootstrap lower expectancy remain screening requirements. The user has specified no numerical maximum drawdown; a live risk budget therefore remains unresolved. Continue in paper mode with no broker orders until the source, profitability, and risk gates are met.

External methods checked: [scikit-learn probability calibration and reliability diagrams](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html), [scikit-learn time-series cross-validation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html#time-series-split), and [Bailey et al. on selecting rules from historical backtests](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1159). These support calibration, chronological validation, and avoiding repeated selection on the opened sample; none establishes MNQ predictability.
