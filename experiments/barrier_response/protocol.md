# Price response when the reservoir's published reversal level is reached

Parent commit 0f32fba. The exact reversal audit found 781 discovery and 1,115 external bars that reached or exceeded the adverse next-close boundary but retained the original sign at the close. The number of future touches is known. Post-event returns for this design have not been scored. Freeze this separate hypothesis now.

Issue an exact boundary after a completed bar only if the parent has a nonzero finite forecast and a positive stock sufficient to reverse it. The next bar must be contiguous. A touch for an originally long sign requires low ≤ boundary, or high ≥ boundary for short. Require next close strictly back on the original side as the primary retained-sign event. For the separately reported crossing event require next close strictly through the boundary. Skip exact ties. A gap across a level still qualifies at the next completed one-minute bar if its timestamps are contiguous; archive a flag if its open was already beyond the threshold. Intrabar high/low are used only after that next bar closes. Orders never fill at the threshold.

Primary policy goes one contract in the original direction after a retained-sign event. The secondary policy goes against the old direction after a crossing. Use distinct standalone strategies. Each flat strategy takes the next qualifying signal, enters at the next open (or one additional minute later), and exits at the open five minutes after entry. Five is the nearest integer half-life of the 7/8 reservoir. Since this exit is on a precommitted clock, an extra minute of entry latency shifts both fills one minute from the corresponding immediate schedule. Reject signals whose predetermined exit time is after the 15:55 New York cutoff. A missing minute cancels a pending entry or flattens an open trade at the first available open, with no interpolation. Once an order is pending or a position is open ignore new signals until flat. An exit at the open can be followed by a fresh decision at that bar's close.

Matched controls: (1) trade the sign of the event candle's body and (2) always go long on the candidate's identical entries/exits; a zero body produces cash and no friction on that row. (3) Build the same reversal boundary from the price-only sibling, run its own primary/secondary events and unrestricted ledger. (4) Cash. No threshold on strength, time-of-day or volume. Score every event at both delays as an overlapping price-response diagnostic and score the separately executable nonoverlapping policies. Report gross and $3/$6/$9 economics, peak-to-trough marked and intrabar drawdown bounds at each cost, concentration, turnover and both source panels. Use five-session paired daily circular-block intervals (10,000 draws, seed 604). For the primary retained-sign policy to advance, require positive $9 net, $6 upper intrabar drawdown below $1,500 and positive lower daily bounds against cash, price sibling, body and always-long controls at $6 in both sources and both delays.
