# Does the volume cell need today's volume at the same minute?

The first study's external fixed-entry next-open volume and price controls disagreed first on 39 of 541 trades. On 34 of those trades volume asked to continue while price wanted to exit. Their mean realized value of holding to the parent exit was negative. Under delayed fills, the profit increment changed sign despite negative mean realized value in this disagreement group. This motivates a time-of-day placebo independent of any new price threshold.

Freeze one diagnostic on the 60 fully gridded and already exposed external sessions. Use dates 6 through 60, with the preceding five full sessions as placebo volume donors. In turn replace only the exit head's volume sequence with each donor's same NY minute sequence and its own causal lagged span-32 EWM. This preserves donor day shape, serial dependence, and complete-session volume profiles. The original entry forecast, entry/exit scale, prices, direction, baseline exit and entry availability use today's real data throughout. A donor session finished before the target opened. Compare actual mass, unit mass, and five donor results; never select the best donor. Use primary identical reservoir entries at both delays and report all 5 lag totals, actual minus equal-weight donor mean, and a 95% circular five-session block bootstrap interval for that paired mean daily difference, 10,000 draws, seed 2711. Include the full strategy as a secondary check of execution and turnover. Cost $6 baseline here, plus $3/$9 on same fills. Publish compact per-day and per-trade results and source checksums.

Tests whether trading decisions benefit specifically from contemporaneous mass beyond the hour-of-day and generic volume variation that survive in past-day controls. A weak result cannot establish absence of volume information in other mechanisms. The parent uses real volume even for the unit-mass exit control. No new market data or candidate training.
