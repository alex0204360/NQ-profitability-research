# Prospective paper test of residual position duration

This registration covers an occupancy estimate for simulated original MNQ reservoir positions. The signed holding-value and downside trading hypotheses failed their exploratory screens. This register issues no trade instructions. Existing prospective reservoir rules, inputs, exclusions and outcomes remain separate.

### Frozen scope

Use the single MNQM27 expiration. Take the first 40 CME US equity-index trading dates scheduled for a full 09:30–16:00 New York window beginning April 1, 2027. Select by the CME holiday calendar before receiving the first bar. The fixed contract and prospective dates avoid roll splices and all observations used for design. Record exchange-sourced, first-received finalized OHLCV and receipt times for that specific contract using one source nominated and recorded before the window. Do not switch feed, fill missing minutes from a later revision or replace failed dates. If the dataset cannot be acquired independently of a protected study, or any of the 40 scheduled sessions has incomplete coverage, report the primary study incomplete. The frozen design creates no scheduled recorder or broker connection.

Generate the unmodified reservoir and first eligible discretionary close at one MNQ, next-open modeled entry/exit and the 15:55 NY cutoff, exactly as on the experimental branch. Start state cold and use the first 20 strictly matured eligible labels as warmup. At each subsequent first eligible decision, issue the unconditional and conditional duration together and retain the pre-label record, input/weight hashes and issuance time. Date, time and timezone refer to minute starts unless explicitly marked as the close. Log all missing, late and revised inputs, including the original recording. A first-issued prediction after the modeled alternative exit cannot demonstrate live timeliness. Report bar-model and timeliness outcomes separately. The original experiment remains unchanged.

The candidate uses the exact formulas frozen at commit 4d6fa93, with the four-sum recurrence in duration.py. The reference is the same recurrence with zero query coordinate, same prior and same clock bound. Features use real first-seen data only, with roughness as in the earlier downside protocol. The label is elapsed minutes from the scheduled alternate executable open to the modeled eventual original exit. Original labels may update the two memories only after the original exit strictly precedes a later decision. Do not refit, mix in historical calibration rows or select a different horizon.

### Decision

The sole primary endpoint is paired mean squared error of log(1 + actual minutes) for the conditioned forecast minus the unconditional forecast, at next-open execution. Require at least 100 scored next-open positions over at least 20 post-warmup sessions and a strictly negative upper endpoint of the 95% paired five-session circular-block percentile interval (10,000 draws, seed 2711, trade-weighted, all post-warmup dates included). If participation is insufficient, report insufficient evidence without extending the window. Repeat with one extra minute as a secondary sensitivity, preserving its own independent cold-start memory. Publish all results including failures. A pass supports duration prediction only. Economic claims would require an independently specified decision policy and a separate test on new observations.
