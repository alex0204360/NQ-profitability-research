# Learn the remaining time before converting it to risk

The frozen diffusion scale ranked residual time weakly yet spanned median predictions of 1 to 49 minutes across extreme quartiles of external next-open trades. Actual medians spanned 13 to 23. Freeze a reward-independent repair. It learns only from elapsed durations of completed original trades. No fit to past downside or trading economics chooses weights.

For a first eligible decision with diffusion duration h and known remaining minutes R, encode u = (h−1)/(h+1) in [0,1). Store four sums for the fixed features 1 and x = 2u−1. At each matured parent exit update sums of [1,x] and [1,x] times log(1+T), where T is the observed number of minutes from the scheduled alternative exit to the eventual original exit. The prior has mass 16 at log(5), corresponding to a four-minute cold start from prior reservoir research. Predict g = (16 log(5)+sum_i (1+x*x_i) log(1+T_i))/(16+sum_i(1+x*x_i)). Convert to minutes H = min(R,max(1,exp(g)−1)). Positive weights 1+x*x_i concentrate on similar past readings, with no fitted threshold. An unconditional duration control sets x to zero at both read and update. A scale-only control uses entry range. Candidate and unconditional-duration controls use entry range times sqrt(H). Keep a separate normalized 80th-percentile tail memory for each scale, with each historical loss divided by its own *issued* scale. Scale feedback and duration feedback both wait until the historical parent exit strictly precedes the current decision. Reset independent memories by source and fill schedule. Identical 20-label warmup and original first-decision ledger to the earlier risk experiment.

Primary forecast scores are 80th-percentile pinball loss relative to range-only and unconditional-duration controls. Separately compare squared error in log(1+T) against the unconditional duration forecast and raw diffusion-time forecast. Report full coverage, mean predictions, all four comparisons, session-paired five-day block intervals, 10,000 draws, seed 2711. Only justify promotion of a duration-conditioned risk challenger if it lowers pinball loss with negative upper bounds against both primary controls in both sources and both fill models. There is no trading policy in this test. Retain scope at exploratory risk diagnostics when the criterion fails.
