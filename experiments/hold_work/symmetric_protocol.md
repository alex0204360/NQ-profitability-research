# Remove the signed bias of provisional repair accounting

First-pass results, read before this revision: the 60-session fixed-entry volume edit improved by $523.50 at next open, but lagged its price control by $350. The full strategy lost $380 more than its parent. The improvement over the price control reverses under one extra minute. All 100 sessions remain exposed.

There is a mathematical reason the uncentered neuron may stop positions even without information about future price. Given its pre-bar state, compare two possible closes displaced from the last close by the same positive magnitude m. Write g for outstanding distance to peak and r for still-provisional repair. The positive branch produces m − ½ min(m,g); the negative branch produces −m − ½ min(m,r). Their mean is −¼[min(m,g)+min(m,r)], always nonpositive. A policy can therefore accumulate negative directional evidence on a fair symmetric price process. This is a statement about the neuron, not about the symmetry of real returns.

Freeze a single correction. At every close remove this conditional mean from the signed pulse before updating memory. All other weights, initialization, entry scale, age, eligibility, controls, filters, friction and fill models are unchanged. The volume cell centers both counterfactuals at the observed volume. Under sign-symmetric innovations conditional on magnitude, volume and past, the new input has zero conditional expectation; the inherited initial prior still decays. Predictive calibration does not follow. No future prices enter either counterfactual.

Enumerate all symmetric unit-step paths of length twelve with normal volume, initial scale two, price zero and horizon exit at last close. Check the signed mean pulse at each depth, the surviving initial prior, first-stop frequency, and whether any causal stop changes expected gross payoff under this martingale. An exit at a close here represents the next opening price with zero overnight gap in the synthetic process. Apply both frozen variants to the same 40 historical and 60 external sessions, on original entries and as full strategies, with both delays. Compare with uncentered and price-only; report interventions, concentration and prediction independent of profitability. No parameter sweeps or promotion on these exposed observations.
