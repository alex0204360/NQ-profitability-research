# Exploratory protocol
Use the canonical Kaggle CSV identified in reports/input_manifest.json, limited to weekday bars end-stamped 09:31–16:00 ET before 2025. Require contiguous history on the same date. Normalize all inputs by mean high-low of 14 bars immediately before the two-bar impulse (fixed before seeing either impulse bar). Use high minus close and close minus low. One range unit of score is assumed to forecast one range unit of movement. Enter long if score times scale exceeds 0.725 points. At the next minute open, hold until close of the fourth minute, one contract, no overlapping positions. Predetermined clock exit is flattened at the last available close on interruption. Costs in points include 0.5 slippage and $4.50 NQ round trip. Compare flat, periodic four-minute longs and two-positive-minute longs at identical costs. Bar-level fills omit queue effects. Use the repository's pre-2025 data even though the earlier neural series explored a longer interval. No claims of reproducing the earlier dollar result.
 
v08 tests overhead inventory. For each of the fourteen closes preceding the pre-impulse anchor, compute positive distance above anchor and average. Subtract this cost from both overload and cap rows before rectification. A move back into prior prices may exhaust its fuel reclaiming those prices. No calibration. Compare with v06 across calendar periods, daily concentration, and one extra tick per fill. Diagnostic matched control: within the same date and clock hour, compare with other two-up decisions failing the v06 gate, closest in normalized impulse amplitude. This uses retrospective control assignment only. Preserve flat unless positive across periods, resilient to extra costs and best-day deletion, with positive incremental evidence.
