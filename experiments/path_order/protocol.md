# Discovery freeze · ordered price and participation

Prior NQ studies found little incremental evidence for a fourteen-level overhead penalty versus unsigned dispersion. In the later study, both a two-close breakout and its compression gate failed costs. Their four-minute forecasts do not answer whether sequencing of volume and price matters. This experiment uses the previously opened September 14 to October 9 MNQ Yahoo tape, full weekdays only. October 12 is a partial terminal session. No oracle, labels, or NQ 2025 holdout. Commit the circuit and simulator before reading any performance.

**Hypothesis.** A burst of unusual participation can precede directional repricing. Conversely, movement followed by growing participation can meet resistance. Weight the two histories with opposite signs. Volume is unsigned, so the neuron can detect a temporal relationship but cannot observe aggressive buys, sells or actual resting liquidity.

The inputs are clipped log2 volume relative to its prior exponential log-volume mean and clipped close displacement relative to prior exponential high–low width. The 1/32 update makes their scale slower than the ordering memory. Clip effort at one doubling/halving, displacement at two ranges. At age one the first bar seeds the background and contributes zero innovations. Reset all state at any missing minute or session boundary. Require 32 bars before an entry.

Let E,Q be the previous 1/8 exponential means of effort e and motion q. Two multiplicative neurons compute E*q and Q*e. Separate recurrent units accumulate each product with retention 7/8. Their difference is an exponentially weighted signed area in effort–motion space. Local increments on a straight line through the origin cancel. Exchanging the order of a pure effort pulse and a pure motion pulse reverses the interaction. The readout assumes one accumulated normalized range per octave is worth one future range. This is an economic hypothesis, not a calibrated conditional mean. Mirror prices around a constant and the score changes sign. Matrices, bias-free recurrences and bounds are in area.json.

**Trading test.** One MNQ at $2/point. Action at completed-minute close; next-bar open is the first simulated fill, with +1 minute for delayed replay. Hold until signed forecast crosses zero or planned 15:55 ET flatten. No predetermined holding horizon. Apply an entry hurdle of 1.5 points ($3 round trip) in all scenarios. Base costs model $2 fees and one $0.50 adverse tick each side. Stress at $6 round trip combines the same fee with four adverse ticks per side. Neither schedule represents a broker quote. Mark after every close and record gap exits at the next available open. Any unresolved gap fill disqualifies validation. No favorable synthetic intrabar fills. Reset features at 09:30 ET. Use a clock window for ordinary weekdays, not an exchange holiday model.

**Controls and falsification.** Run cash, eight-bar leaky momentum (8Q), and an unsigned-order ablation (same product gates with readout [1,1], symmetric in the two input streams). At each circuit trade also use the sign of momentum at that same decision, keeping identical entry/exit times and costs. Report realized net, trade expectancy, marked drawdown, delay and cost sensitivity, concentration and paired daily differences. Inspect signed forward open-to-open returns at fixed 1, 8 and 24 bars on contiguous windows only, as explanatory labels outside the brain. Treat small-sample intervals as descriptive. At most one mechanistically motivated redesign on this discovery tape. Preserve both frozen circuits and all failures.

**Validation track.** After all designs and the validation protocol are frozen, attempt one read of November 16–20 2026 daytime MNQ, never used in this discovery. Prefer explicitly dated December MNQ over a continuous symbol. Paired cash, symmetric and momentum controls, identical execution, no edits on that panel. A favorable week alone cannot qualify a discovery. A validated claim requires documented source identity, timestamps, execution and independent provenance, at least 20 new sessions and 50 trades, positive stressed net at both delays, positive paired block interval against cash and momentum, and zero unresolved gap trades. When identity or provenance is ambiguous, label a later replay temporal replication only. If another candidate follows exposure, all these dates are discovery for it.
