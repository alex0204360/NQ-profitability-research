How the neuron works

Charge is the positive part of volume surprise minus the size of the close-to-close move, with each input measured against its recent baseline. Charge adds to stock. Later moves release some of that stock in the direction of the move. A move of one recent range or more spends the entire available stock, and each portion can be spent only once. Each minute carries forward 7/8 of the unspent stock and 7/8 of the prior directional state. The plot shows the forecast in MNQ points. Stock, charge, and signed release are internal values.
