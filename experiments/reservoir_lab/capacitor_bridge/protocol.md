# Capacitor under load

The original reservoir injects a signed release and exponentially carries it whether the subsequent price has already moved in its direction or not. Model a *finite remaining displacement* in MNQ points. At the close of each minute transfer the directed release into a capacitor. On the next completed bar, discharge the old potential by at most the favorable price travel, never by more than the stored magnitude. Adverse travel cannot recharge it. Leak the remainder and inject the new release at its observed point scale. When the bar sequence breaks, reset. This is a hypothesis about the units and timing of market information, not a literal law of market physics.
