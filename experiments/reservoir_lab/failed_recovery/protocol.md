# Failed recovery attempt: discrete episode memory

Parent experiment's four-bar debt forecasts averaged −22.97 points on November exit readings but the corresponding continuing trades subsequently gained +1.23 points on average. The circuit reacted to an adverse displacement before price had shown whether recovery was possible. The new hypothesis requires a sequence: a growing adverse bar, a recovery attempt, then a close through that same shock's terminal close. This is a second exploratory design informed by the first results on all 34 dates.

Use direction-relative prices (long uses price, short uses minus price). On a bar with positive excess under both frozen size and volume-adjusted impact comparisons, remember its preceding close U, ending close L and span U−L. While waiting for a recovery, replace the episode on a further qualifying lower shock close. Any close at or above U cancels the episode. A later close at least halfway from L to U arms a retest memory. After arming, observe the greatest favorable close P. A *subsequent* close strictly below L emits one failure pulse P minus the current direction-relative close, in points. It clears the memory immediately. One shock can charge the readout only once. Roll the directional memories continuously on completed bars and reset at every missing minute. Midpoint is the symmetry point of the observed leg, independent of any selected trade's P&L. Fixed numerical matrices in weights.json implement comparisons and readout; recurrent gates route the observed prices to sample-and-hold memories.

H = (1−(7/8)^4) × side × original forecast − failure pulse. At failure one further leg of the observed failed recovery's size is hypothesized over the same four upcoming open-to-open intervals capped by the original exit. Positive favors continuing either side; nonpositive requests next-open exit. No pulse gives the original zero-crossing exit. Price-only sets relative volume to one in the trigger. All other policies, panels, cost schedule, chart cases and attribution follow the first experiment. Freeze before replays, preserve both outcomes and keep November 23 separate. No threshold sweep.
