Inside the neuron

Charge measures unusual volume absorbed with little price movement. Stock holds unused charge. Signed release sends part of that stock into the forecast as price moves. New release plus the carried forecast equals the forecast in points, before rounding.

After 32 consecutive bars, a forecast beyond ±1.5 points can open a position. A change of sign exits it. Positions flatten at the 15:55 New York open.
