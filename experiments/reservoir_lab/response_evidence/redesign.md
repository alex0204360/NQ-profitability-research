# Designs locked after first discovery replay, before external outcomes

In 299 fixed-entry trades the evidence circuit never fired. The maximum pooled evidence was 5.55 in the earlier 20 sessions and 5.75 in November. On the November 19 long it peaked at 1.52. Favorable excursions reduce a cell's capital multiplicatively and can make a late change of direction difficult to detect. Retain the original boundary, three stakes, and control. Apply one rectifier per cell before each update: wealth = 1 + ReLU(wealth - 1). This implements the best product of increments over suffixes (including a fresh start immediately before this bar). The wealth can now repeatedly restart, so the original per-trade martingale false-alarm bound no longer applies. Compare restart against original, unrestarted and its own volume-off control. Identical-entry and full re-entry tests as before. The chart's example buttons refer only to genuine paired improvement and damage under the restart edit.

A separately declared low-turnover alternative addresses a different question: did transacted volume accept the opening move through the afternoon? The idea draws on Gao et al.'s first-to-last half-hour ETF momentum, but here opening-to-10:00 replaces previous-close-to-10:00, the cutoff is 15:55, and the instrument is MNQ. [Original study](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/market-intraday-momentum/)

Record d = 09:59 close minus 09:30 open. Project every subsequent typical price ((H+L+C)/3) into the two-endpoint coordinate clip(2*(P-open)/d - 1,-1,1). A trade at the opening level votes -1, a trade at the endpoint votes +1; individual trade locations are approximated by each minute's typical price. Accumulate signed votes weighted by minute volume, dividing by total volume. At the 15:29 close cap the positive mean by the same coordinate of current close, clipped to [0,1]. Output d × 25/30 × that share: a hypothesized repeat of the accepted part of opening velocity during the 25 tradable closing minutes. Score strictly beyond ±3 points permits one contract at 15:30 open, flat at 15:55. Missing observation before decision invalidates that day's activation. No overnight information. Price-only sets all masses equal. Also score naked opening direction using the same 3-point hurdle and unconditional long over the same clock on eligible days. Never scale a second contract into the result. Exact operations and weights in auction.py. No fitted coefficients.

Test the same external screen for all three candidate families. For the auction demand positive lower paired bounds against opening-only and always-long as well. Publish all outcomes. A positive result under weak controls does not promote any candidate. No external-data redesign in this experiment.
