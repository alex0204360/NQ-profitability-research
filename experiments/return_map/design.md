# Price-addressed participation memory

## Hypothesis before the first score

The scalar reservoir can release the same forecast in two markets with different paths relative to the original deposit price. A local revisit offers a way to distinguish those histories. If price traveled one original range from a high-participation, low-displacement close and subsequently approached that close without closing through it, the first excursion may be recoverable. The sign is the direction of that excursion. OHLCV supplies a price coordinate and a participation proxy. It supplies no resting orders.

Each positive deposit occupies one of 32 rotating memory slots for at most 32 completed bars, one background update time. Each slot stores its close, prior range scale, mass, direction and phase. All price distances in that slot use its original scale. The frozen parent encoder computes relative log volume and normalized close displacement. The rectified charge weights are [1, −1, −1] on effort and the two motion hinges. A close at least one scale from the deposit latches its direction. The first later close back within half a scale activates that site if it is still on the departure side of the anchor. An overshoot through the anchor extinguishes it. Close-only transitions avoid making an assumption about intrabar ordering.

A live site votes for the signed distance remaining to the one-range departure level. Touching that level, closing through the anchor, reaching the age limit or a gap extinguishes the vote. Sum votes weighted by deposit mass and divide by the larger of one and total live mass. This bounds the forecast by the largest remaining distance while allowing weak deposits to abstain. The equal-mass control assigns 0.25 octave of charge to every positive deposit, preserving all sites and their entire transition history. It tests whether measured mass adds value, and can change trade frequency. Matrix rows, biases and the exact recurrence are in weights.json and circuit.py.

Use the parent's 32-bar warmup, fixed 1.5-point entry hurdle, sign crossing exit, 15:55 ET flatten, gap execution and one MNQ contract. Compare cash, reservoir and equal-mass readouts at the next open and one more minute, with $3 and $6 round trips. Compute close-marked drawdown including the initial zero. Keep account controls outside the model. No tuning or sign reversal for a losing backtest. Inspect contacts, overshoots, site lifetimes and economics to identify any distinct computation justified by a failure. A challenger merits a prospective freeze only if both source periods show positive stressed economics at both delays, no marked drawdown above $1,500 on the combined history, and evidence of incremental value over its control. Any qualifying protocol must be registered separately before new observations. All current sessions are exploratory.

## Surgery decision after scoring the discovery panel

At first contact the observer has seen a return *toward* a site. It has seen no evidence that the site pushed price away. A straight-through path and a rebound can have identical prefixes at that instant. On the discovery panel 215 of 220 immediate entries occurred on a flat or incoming close relative to the trade side. By the next close 75 of 220 signals had lost their direction. The two-minute median hold makes the extra minute of latency material. Gross was $1,214 before $660 base costs and $1,320 stressed costs. These facts motivate one additional phase, without changing the charge, eligibility, target, or execution. The counts at later closes are evaluation labels only.

For each live site, store its previous oriented displacement. A threshold neuron with weights [1, −1] on current and previous displacement, strict threshold zero, latches the first subsequent outbound close strictly inside the original live interval. A confirmed site then votes by the original distance and mass until a pre-existing invalidation occurs. A one-close excursion through the target cannot confirm. A synthetic straight-through approach must never fire. Compare the gated head to both its equal-mass partner and the ungated head. Freeze this revision before viewing any November challenger P&L. If confirmation simply consumes the distance available for execution, the economic experiment can reject it.
